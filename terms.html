Subscription terms

ResetPlan Premium is an auto-renewing subscription offered through the App Store.

Billing

The App Store displays the current price and billing period before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Apple charges for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.

Cancellation

You can manage or cancel a subscription in App Store settings or from the Premium screen. Deleting ResetPlan does not cancel an App Store subscription.

What Premium includes

Unlimited routines, 26 weeks of reflection history, and up to three weekly observations. Core task planning, the scheduler, Daily Reset, notifications, data export, and the free reflection view do not require a subscription.

Restoring and refunds

Use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen to restore an active subscription associated with your Apple Account. Purchases are verified with StoreKit; tapping a purchase button alone never unlocks Premium. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store terms.

Apple's standard EULA also applies: apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula.
