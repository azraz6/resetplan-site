ResetPlan

Privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version

ResetPlan is local-first. Task content, routines, notes and history are stored in the app's local database on your device. We do not operate a ResetPlan account service, analytics backend, advertising network, or remote AI service. Purchases are handled by Apple.

What is stored and why

Tasks, subtasks, deadlines, schedules, routines, routine completions, Daily Reset summaries, planned breaks, weekly notes and preferences are stored so the planner can work offline and survive relaunches. A local entitlement cache records whether Apple has verified an active subscription so the interface doesn't flicker while StoreKit refreshes. It contains no payment credentials.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, ResetPlan asks iOS for permission and schedules local notifications for scheduled tasks, routines, daily planning and weekly reflection. Reminder text may include task or routine titles. You can hide task titles in Reminder settings. Quiet hours skip reminders that would fire inside those hours; skipped reminders are not delivered later. Apple's notification system delivers the notifications.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple using StoreKit. ResetPlan never receives your payment card or Apple Account password. Product details, purchase verification and subscription status come from Apple. Apple's privacy policy and App Store terms apply. Deleting local app data does not cancel a subscription.

External processing

The current version does not send task content to any AI provider, does not access your calendar, and does not contain third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If a future version adds a remote feature, it will require clear disclosure and user consent before task content is transmitted, and this policy will be updated.

Backups and device services

Depending on your iPhone or iPad settings, iOS may include app data in device backups or device-to-device transfers. Those copies are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by ResetPlan. ResetPlan does not provide its own cloud sync in this version. Deleting data inside ResetPlan cannot reach into existing device backups.

Security

Data is stored in the app container and protected by iOS platform protections such as device passcode and Data Protection. ResetPlan does not claim end-to-end encryption or a separate encryption layer. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or an unencrypted backup may be able to read app data.

Your choices

You can edit or delete individual tasks and routines, export all local data as JSON from Data & privacy, disable reminders, and delete all local data from Settings. Deletion is immediate in the app. There is no remote account to delete.

Children and medical claims

ResetPlan is a general planning tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose ADHD or any condition, and does not replace medical or psychological care.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact support@resetplan.app. The developer must keep this address and the public privacy-policy URL current before App Store submission.
