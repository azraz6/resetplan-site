Support

ResetPlan is a calm, flexible planner. If something isn't working, start with the answers below.

Why didn't all my tasks fit?

Daily Reset respects your planning window, fixed-time tasks, task durations, deadlines, breaks and buffer. Tasks that don't fit are never deleted. They stay in your list with a reason, and you can plan them later.

Why did a reminder not arrive?

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → ResetPlan and the Reminder settings in the app. Quiet hours deliberately skip reminders inside that window instead of delivering them later. Task reminders require a scheduled start time.

What happens when I remove a task from today?

It leaves today's plan but remains in your task list. Deleting a task is a separate action and asks for confirmation. A Daily Reset can be undone from Today.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited routines, 26 weeks of reflection history and deeper weekly observations. Tasks, scheduling, Daily Reset, reminders, export and the free reflection view work without a subscription.

How do I manage a subscription?

Open Settings → ResetPlan Premium, or use App Store subscription settings. You can also use Restore Purchases there. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Data, export & privacy → Delete all local data. This removes local tasks, routines, history, notes and preferences. It cannot cancel an App Store subscription and cannot remove copies from iOS device backups.

Contact

Open a support request at github.com/azraz6/resetplan-site/issues. Please include your iOS version and a short description; don't include task content unless it is necessary. Support requests are public, so never post sensitive information.
